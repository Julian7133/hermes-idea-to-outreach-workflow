# 00 — Idea Capture

## Raw idea
A niche inventory + livestock management SaaS for independent reptile and exotic-pet
stores. Tracks live animals (species, morph, sex, acquisition cost, feeding
schedule, health notes), dry goods (feeders, substrate, enclosures), and
supplier orders in one place — replacing the spreadsheets and paper logs most
small reptile shops use today. Built for the owner-operator of a 1–3 location shop.

## Monetization assumption
Per-account subscription, ~$49–$99/mo flat (single location), +$29/mo per extra
location. One owner pays; 1–4 staff seats included. `ASSUMPTION:` WTP anchored to
the cost of one hour/week of the owner's time saved on stock-taking.

## Micro-SaaS fit
Narrow, unsexy vertical (reptile retail is a small, ignored niche), recurring
operational pain (livestock mortality and mis-orders cost real money), buildable
solo (CRUD + scheduling + simple reporting), and a reachable ICP (independent
shops are listified via directories, breeder expos, and niche Facebook groups).
Poor-fit risk: the total number of shops is small, so SOM is capped.

## Trigger / why now
Reptile keeping grew during 2020–2023; a wave of small shops opened and are now
professionalising. Generic retail POS (Square, Lightspeed) does not model live
animals (morph genetics, feeding, mortality), so owners bolt on spreadsheets.
`ASSUMPTION:` cheap web hosting + no-code-ish stacks make a vertical tool
economically buildable for a solo founder.

## Open questions
- How many independent reptile/exotic shops exist in the target region (SOM cap)?
- Do owners already pay for any software beyond POS?
- Is livestock tracking painful enough to switch from spreadsheets?
